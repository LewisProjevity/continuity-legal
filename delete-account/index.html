Account Deletion

LAST UPDATED: 19 SEPTEMBER 2026

Continuity is operated by Projevity Pty Ltd (ACN 696 573 154).

Deleting your account permanently removes your Continuity account and associated cloud data. This action cannot be undone.

Delete in the app

You can delete your account from inside Continuity at any time:

SETTINGS → DELETE ACCOUNT

If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more so that we can revoke the Sign in with Apple authorisation, as Apple requires.

Or request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, you can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data.

Email a deletion request

Send it from the email address on your account, or tell us the email address Apple shows for you. For security reasons, we may need to confirm that you are the account holder before deleting the account. We complete verified requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

When a deletion request is confirmed, we delete or anonymise:

  • account information
  • cloud progress, rank and streaks
  • badges and lifetime statistics
  • nightly results and casebook progress
  • hunches, leader bets and community statistics entries associated with your account

Aggregated statistics that no longer identify you may remain. Copies in technical backups are removed within a reasonable period. Any information that we are legally required to retain will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the Privacy Policy.

Guest data

Guest progress is stored only on your device and is not affected by account deletion. Uninstalling Continuity or clearing its app data may remove guest progress.

Need help?

Email support@projevity.com.au for assistance with a deletion request.
